**Hello, World!**

*Every project starts somewhere, and this is where mine begins.*

I've always wanted to create a personal blog. A place to document my work, share what I've learned, write about my interests, and keep track of the ideas and experiences I encounter along the way.

I've tried creating one several times before

I've tried creating one several times before, experimenting with different platforms, designs, and made several attempts to bring the ideas come to life. Somehow, I never quite managed to get one off the ground.

But the idea never really went away. This time, I'm giving it another shot with **STRAY SIGNALS**.


## What Is STRAY SIGNALS?

STRAY SIGNALS is my personal space for exploring, documenting, and sharing the things that interest me. It brings together my experiences, projects, technical discoveries, research, and reflections across different areas of technology and beyond. Think of it as a combination of a professional portfolio, technical archive, and personal journal. It's a place for the systems I build, the knowledge I acquire, the ideas I explore, and the experiences that shape my perspective.

I'll be sharing topics around IT operations, infrastructure, management, cybersecurity, research, personal projects, and other interests as they develop. Some posts will be technical documentation or tutorials; others might be learning notes, project updates, external resources, or simply thoughts worth putting into writing.

If you'd like to learn more about the story and inspiration behind this site, you can visit the [About page](/about/).


## What to Expect

There isn't a strict direction or a fixed set of topics I'm committing to. I want this blog to grow naturally alongside my interests, projects, and experiences. I intend to share content across different areas, including:

* **IT Operations & Infrastructure** — Notes, projects, configurations, troubleshooting experiences, and lessons from working with IT systems and infrastructure.
* **Cybersecurity & Threat Intelligence** — Learning materials, investigations, technical exercises, case analyses, and insights from my cybersecurity journey.
* **Technology & Research** — Topics I'm exploring, concepts I'm trying to understand, useful resources, and research worth documenting.
* **Projects & Experiments** — Systems I build, tools I try, ideas I work on, and things I create along the way.
* **Learning & Documentation** — Technical notes, tutorials, guides, study journals, and lessons learned from both successes and mistakes.
* **Thoughts & Reflections** — Personal experiences, observations, interesting discoveries, and occasional thoughts beyond technology.
* **Open-source Softwares & Systems** - Open-source tools, platforms, and systems I use, explore, configure, or experiment with.
* **External Publications** - Articles, publications, and other works contributed to or published through my professional work.

These are not fixed categories or limitations. Some posts may be technical and detailed, while others may simply document an idea, an experience, or something worth remembering.

## What's Ahead?

I'll do my best to keep this blog updated as I continue learning, working on projects, and exploring new interests. There may not always be a fixed publishing schedule, but I hope to make this a place where I can consistently document what I'm working on.

Not every post will be perfect, and not every idea will be fully developed. Some entries may start as simple notes and eventually grow into more detailed documentation. Others may be updated as I gain new knowledge, discover better approaches, or revisit topics with a different perspective. 

For me, the important thing is to start, keep creating, and have somewhere to look back on how far I've come. More importantly, I want this to be an honest record of progress, not just the finished results, but also the process, challenges, experiments, and lessons behind them.

I don't have everything planned out, and that's part of the point. There are still plenty of things to learn, build, investigate, and experience. I don't know exactly where this will lead, and that's perfectly fine. For now, I'll take it one post, one project, and one discovery at a time.

**This blog will evolve as I do, and I look forward to seeing where these interests lead.**

This is another beginning, but hopefully one that lasts.

Whether you're here for technical documentation, see a glimpse into what I'm currently exploring, or just randomly wandered in this blog. I hope you find things here useful.

**Welcome to STRAY SIGNALS.** \
